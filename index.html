Meech Card Co. Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

1. Overview

Meech Card Co. operates an application that connects with eBay through eBay's authorized APIs to help manage sports card inventory, listings, sales information, and related business operations.

2. Information Accessed

When authorized by the account owner, the application may access information made available through eBay's APIs, including information relating to listings, inventory, orders, sales, and other information necessary to operate the application.

3. How Information Is Used

Information obtained through eBay APIs is used solely for legitimate Meech Card Co. business operations, including inventory management, listing management, sales tracking, analytics, and related business functions.

4. Information Sharing

Meech Card Co. does not sell personal information obtained through eBay APIs. Information is not shared with third parties except where necessary to operate authorized services, comply with legal obligations, protect the application, or fulfill the purposes described in this policy.

5. Data Security

Meech Card Co. takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information accessed through the application. API credentials and authentication tokens are treated as confidential credentials and are not intentionally exposed publicly.

6. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate Meech Card Co.'s inventory, sales, accounting, analytics, and historical business records, subject to applicable legal and contractual requirements.

7. Account Access and Revocation

The eBay account owner may revoke the application's authorization through eBay. Upon revocation, the application will no longer be authorized to access the account through the revoked authorization.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as the application and its business functions evolve. Any updated version will be published at this URL.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions regarding Meech Card Co. or this application, please use the contact information associated with the Meech Card Co. eBay account.
